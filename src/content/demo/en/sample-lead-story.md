## A site ready before its first article

The template shows these sample articles until the site has its own, so you can see at once how the home page, a section and an article page look. There is no need to delete them: they disappear with the first real article of each language.

## What decides the look

Name, sections, languages, colours and one of ten styles are chosen in a single file, site.config.mjs. Every change is a commit and the site rebuilds itself on Vercel or Netlify.

## Where the articles come from

Redazia writes each article as a Markdown file in the folder of its language, with title, description, date, section and cover. It can do so when you press Publish, or on its own with the automatic newsroom.
