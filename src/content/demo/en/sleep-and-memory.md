## The tidy-up

In deep sleep the brain replays the day's experiences and strengthens the ones that matter.

## Why sleep before an exam

Studying and then sleeping helps you remember better than staying up all night with the books.

## How much sleep

Recommendations for adults usually say between seven and nine hours a night.
