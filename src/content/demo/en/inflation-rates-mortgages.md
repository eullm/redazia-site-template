## The first link

Inflation measures how much prices rise on average. To keep it near its target, a central bank can make money dearer by raising interest rates.

## The second link

Policy rates feed into the indices that variable-rate mortgages are tied to. When the index rises, so does the payment.

## What to watch

Anyone with a variable mortgage can follow the central bank's decisions and compare the cost of switching to a fixed rate.
