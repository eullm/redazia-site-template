## What changes

A passkey is a pair of cryptographic keys: the private one stays on the device, the site keeps only the public one. There is nothing to remember and nothing a fake site can trick you into handing over.

## How to turn it on

Services that support them list them among the security settings. The device asks for a fingerprint, face or PIN, and the key is saved in the system's keychain.

## What if I lose my phone?

Passkeys sync across the devices of the same account. It is still wise to keep a second way in, such as a physical security key.
