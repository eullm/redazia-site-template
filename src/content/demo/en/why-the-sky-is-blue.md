## Light gets scattered

Air molecules scatter light, and they do it far more with short wavelengths, like blue, than with long ones, like red. This is Rayleigh scattering.

## And sunsets?

At sunset light crosses much more atmosphere before reaching our eyes. The blue is scattered along the way and mostly orange and red remain.

## Why not violet?

Violet scatters even more, but the Sun emits less of it and our eyes are less sensitive to that colour.
