## The maths

At an average reading pace, twenty minutes a day make several thousand pages a year: enough for many books.

## The trick

Tying reading to a moment that already exists, like the morning coffee or the train ride, makes it automatic.

## What to read

Alternating a demanding book with a light one helps you keep going on tired days.
